Squircle tiles

Today's set, for comparison. Each icon sits on the navy aurora squircle.

01

Glass Drop

Clear glass with no tile. The shape is the icon. The aurora bends through it, like the Drifter drop logo.

Glass, refraction, aurora
02

Frosted Panes

A color slab with a frosted pane floating in front of it, offset. The glyph sits on the frost. It uses the same idea as Drifter's frosted panels.

Frost, layers, depth
03

Soft Clay

Thick matte forms with soft light. It is calm and friendly, and the shapes are easy to read at small sizes.

Matte, puffy, quiet
04

Enamel Pin

Glossy enamel color inside raised silver lines, cut to the shape. It feels like a badge you could pin on a bag.

Metal, gloss, crafted
05

Night Neon

Glowing tubes that trace the outline. It is made for the dark desktop and the aurora wallpaper.

Light, outline, night
06

Isometric Block

Thick blocks seen from above at an angle, like objects on a map. It fits Drifter's idea of the desktop as a place you look down on.

Geography, solid, map
07

Topo Strata

The shape is built from stacked contour layers, like a terrain map, from dark at the base to light at the top. It matches the Topo wallpaper.

Contours, terrain, layers
08

Smoked Glass

Dark tinted glass with light in its edges, like edge-lit acrylic. The glyph glows from inside. It is the darkest style here.

Dark, edge light, glow
09

Porcelain Glaze

White glossy ceramic with an inlay of colored glaze and a thin white rim. It is clean and works best on the light theme.

Ceramic, white, glaze
10

Aurora Line

Flat. Thin rounded lines with a soft tint fill and a slight glow. It is the one 2D style, for people who want less depth.

Flat, line, light
